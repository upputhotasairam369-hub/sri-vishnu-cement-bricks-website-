How We Make It

Our Quality
Manufacturing Process

Every brick passes through a rigorous five-stage process to ensure you get a consistent, strong product — every single time.

1

Raw Material Selection

Only grade-A cement and well-graded aggregates are accepted on site

2

Precise Batching

Cement-to-aggregate ratios weighed accurately for every batch

3

Machine Pressing

Hydraulic press compacts each brick to a uniform density and dimension

4

Water Curing

28-day water-curing cycle develops full compressive strength

5

Quality Check

Final inspection for size, strength and surface quality before dispatch
